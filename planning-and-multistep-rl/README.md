# Multi-Step Reinforcement Learning and Planning

Implementation and experimental analysis of multi-step and model-based reinforcement learning methods in a 5×20 Cliff Walking environment.

## Methods

- n-Step SARSA
- Dyna-Q
- Dyna-Q+
- Model-Based Planning
- Exploration in Non-Stationary Environments

## Experiments

### n-Step SARSA
Comparison of n-step SARSA with n = 1, 5, and 10 across multiple independent runs.

### Dyna-Q
Evaluation of Dyna-Q with different numbers of planning steps (0, 5, and 10).

### Dyna-Q+
Comparison of Dyna-Q and Dyna-Q+ in a dynamically changing Cliff Walking environment to study adaptation and exploration after environmental changes.
